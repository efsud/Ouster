How the model works
Each cell can send vehicles downstream only when the next cell has space. Red signals stop flow across the intersection boundary. Unserved demand stays in an upstream waiting queue, so vehicles are conserved.
flow = min(sending capacity, receiving capacity)
vehicles next = vehicles now + inflow − outflow
This first model has one through lane and no turning movements. Calibration, turning flows, lane groups, and observed signal plans are required before field validation.

Before publishing a field KPI
Verify sensor origin and rotation, lane and queue geometry, timestamps, physical-track deduplication, and signal phase mapping. Require at least 15 valid traversals and 80% coverage per reporting window. Validate against independent counts and controller logs.
