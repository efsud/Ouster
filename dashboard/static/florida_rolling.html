How groups help reconstruction · validation

Groups contain observed individual trajectories with similar road paths, passage times and speed profiles. Selecting a group keeps its individual trajectories; no average line replaces them. A group does not establish vehicle identity. Missing portions are estimated separately for each vehicle only when the reconstruction method passes recorded-data tests.
