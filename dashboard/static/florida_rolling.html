From means the side the vehicle comes from. N/S are provisional road-relative side-street arms. Unknown tracks are not linked across sensors. Hover over a trajectory for its time and speed.

Red = slow, yellow = about 10 m/s, green = 20+ m/s. These are speed colors, not signal states. Samples are taken at up to 2 Hz. Short gaps (1.5–3 seconds) use endpoint interpolation. Vehicle gaps up to 30 seconds use movement before and after the gap, subject to speed, acceleration, direction and clock checks. Spatial blind-band connections are separate, experimental cross-sensor association hypotheses using visible endpoint time, speed and class. They are not verified vehicle identities. Ambiguous candidates stay blank. Local time gaps over 30 seconds remain blank; spatial connections use a separate maximum travel time of 120 seconds. These estimates are motion-based reconstructions, not DeepCTM forecasts. Unknown cross-sensor clock offsets and turning traffic may invalidate spatial connections.
